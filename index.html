    Full is the cinematic look; Balanced drops the depth of field; Light draws without shadows and is easiest on the computer. Until you choose, the stage steps down on its own when frames fall behind.

    Agents

    An agent with the connection string sees what you see and can change it: the moment, the tempo, the camera, the fingering. The one in the box at the bottom left of the piano has the same powers, and talks.

    Notation

    Faint lines mark the first two ledger lines under each staff: C and A below the treble, E and C below the bass.

    Keys

    ← and → move through the piece, Home and End go to its ends, P plays it, Space hears the moment again (Shift for one finger at a time), N shows or hides the finger numbers, F is full screen, L starts a lesson, G is “Got it” and V held down is the microphone, for the agent or the teacher. Drag the piano to turn the camera, 1 to 9 for your saved views, C for auto cut. Click a key to play it, or a measure to go there.
